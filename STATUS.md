# Motif build update

**Works now (in automated tests):** a person rests for 10 seconds and picks a comfortable movement, or lets Motif suggest movements after moving freely. They practise it, pass separate checks and an "everyday activity" check, and approve it. That movement alone can then pick phrases, build and speak a message, pause, change speed and recalibrate. Up to four movements can work together. A setup can be saved on this computer and restored later after a quick re-check. Motif shows plain counts of what it saw, suggests which movement should do what, and lets a person say a movement is tiring. If the face is lost or the tab is hidden, nothing gets selected. A Space-bar mode is there for people who can't use a face movement.

**Also checked automatically:** two different people using one computer, noisy signals, lighting changes, flickering tracking, camera and model errors, blocked storage, no speech voice, keyboard focus, text contrast and size.

**Being checked now:** a final independent review of this polish work.

**Still needs a person:** the camera is covered, so no human has tried real face tracking, comfort, spoken audio or hands-free use yet. `ACCEPTANCE.md` lists exactly what to try.

How to run it: `README.md`. Exact restart steps: `progress.md`.
