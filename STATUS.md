# Motif build update

**Works now (in automated tests):** a person rests for 10 seconds, picks a comfortable movement, practices it, passes separate checks and an "everyday activity" check, and approves it. That movement alone can then pick phrases, build and speak a message, pause, change speed and recalibrate. Up to four movements can work together, each checked so it doesn't get in the way of the others. A setup can be saved on this computer and used again later, but only after a fresh rest check and a quick re-check of each movement. If the face is lost or the tab is hidden, nothing gets selected. A Space-bar mode is there for people who can't use a face movement.

**Being checked:** the new "how did my movements do" summary. It shows plain counts, suggests which movement should do what, and lets a person say a movement is tiring.

**Next:** finding movements by just moving freely, then final polish.

**Still needs a person:** the camera is covered, so no human has tried real face tracking, comfort, spoken audio or hands-free use yet.

Exact restart steps are in `progress.md`.
